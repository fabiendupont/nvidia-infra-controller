Machines without the provisioner annotation use NICo Core gRPC (default).
To route a machine through Metal3 Bare Metal Operator, add the annotation
to its expectedMachine resource before NICo discovers the physical machine:

  kubectl patch expectedmachine <name> --type=merge \
    -p '{"metadata":{"annotations":{"nico.nvidia.com/provisioner":"metal3"}}}'

Or declare it in the expectedMachine manifest:
  metadata:
    annotations:
      nico.nvidia.com/provisioner: metal3
